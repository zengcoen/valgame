A Magical Journey

A Romance Adventure

By Coen

They say a legendary letter lies hidden along a path few ever find. An old scrap, folded and worn. It reads:

Ride before sunset. Follow the marked winds.
What was once unsaid waits to be found.

You've heard the tale. Tonight, you've come to seek it.

Ah, welcome. I'm the stable master. I've been tending these horses and these trails for more years than I care to count.

You've heard about the letter, the one hidden along the paths. Many have searched. Few have gotten far. You'll need a companion. Three horses graze beyond: Ember, Mist, and Star. Each has a soul as distinct as the seasons. One of them will carry you where you need to go. Trust your heart when you choose.

The stable master has pointed you toward the meadow. Three horses graze in the twilight, each one waiting, each one watching. One of them will carry you along the trails in search of the hidden letter. Choose wisely.

Choose Your Horse

Ember

Spirited and swift. A fiery heart that loves to run. For those who seek warmth and excitement.

Mist

Gentle and wise. Carries an air of ancient knowing. For the curious and dreamy soul.

Star

Bright and steadfast. Guides by starlight. For those who believe in wonder.

A fine choice. A fine choice indeed.

A name holds power. Give your companion a name, one that speaks true. Ride before sunset. The trails are waiting. And so, I've heard, is someone else who's been searching for the same letter. Good luck out there.
